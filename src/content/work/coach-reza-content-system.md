## The project

Sample project: a personal-brand system for a fitness coach selling online coaching — education that proves expertise, quotes that carry the brand voice, and offer posts that convert. **This is a concept project. No client, no live account, and no growth to report. What is real is the planning work below.**

- **Target:** Indonesian fitness beginners and busy professionals, 18–40, who want fat loss and body recomposition.
- **Platform:** Instagram Feed & Reels.
- **Goals:** Personal brand authority, online coaching enrollment, community consistency.

## Content pillars

- **Education.** Form checks with visual callouts — what correct looks like and why.
- **Motivation.** Bold quote graphics in the coach's voice, placed on the grid diagonal.
- **Offer.** Online coaching tiers (Basic / Premium / VIP) with clear inclusions per tier.
- **Proof.** Before-and-after transformations with timeframe stated.
- **Nutrition.** High-protein, budget-friendly meal education.
- **Behind the coach.** Certifications, experience, and the face behind the program.

## What this sample shows

How a black-and-yellow high-energy system stays readable across education, offer, and proof posts — and how pricing content can sit in the feed without breaking its rhythm. Planning work is real; numbers are not claimed.
