## The project

Sample project: a content system for an online education brand that has to do two jobs at once — give away genuinely useful tips and sell bootcamps. **This is a concept project. No client, no live account, and no growth to report. What is real is the planning work below.**

- **Target:** Indonesian students and career switchers, 18–30, looking for job-ready skills.
- **Platform:** TikTok Feed.
- **Goals:** Brand awareness, course enrollment, saves and shares on tips content.

## Content pillars

- **Tips.** Study and Excel shortcuts formatted for saves — checklist layout, one promise per post.
- **Offers.** Bootcamp and course posts with curriculum, mentor, and certificate proof points.
- **Testimonials.** Alumni quotes with concrete outcomes (interviews, hired, timeframe).
- **Career advice.** Portfolio and skill-demand posts that position the brand as the guide.
- **Behind the scenes.** Mentor and production shots that prove course quality.
- **Flexibility.** Learn-anywhere messaging with app and community proof.

## What this sample shows

How value-first tips content and direct course offers share one bold visual system without the feed reading as an ad wall — and how testimonials carry specific outcomes instead of vague praise. Planning work is real; numbers are not claimed.
