## The project

Sample project: a content system for a preloved fashion shop where trust is the product — buyers need to believe the quality, the fit, and the seller before they tap order. **This is a concept project. No client, no live account, and no growth to report. What is real is the planning work below.**

- **Target:** Indonesian fashion lovers, 18–30, hunting branded quality on a budget.
- **Platform:** TikTok Feed.
- **Goals:** Weekly drop sell-through, buyer trust, repeat customers.

## Content pillars

- **Drops.** Haul and Friday-drop announcements with price, schedule, and urgency.
- **Styling.** One-item-three-outfits challenges that prove preloved versatility.
- **Process.** Curation and packing behind-the-scenes — quality checks buyers can see.
- **Education.** Thrifting tips (fabric, fit, timeless colours) that position the seller as expert.
- **Proof.** Before-after transformations and buyer testimonials with concrete details.
- **Brand.** Style-on-a-budget statements that make thrift feel aspirational, not cheap.

## What this sample shows

How a playful pastel system carries both urgency (drop schedules) and reassurance (packing, testimonials) — and how a weekly drop rhythm gives the whole feed a heartbeat. Planning work is real; numbers are not claimed.
