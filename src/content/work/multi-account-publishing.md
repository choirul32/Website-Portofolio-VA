## The situation

The agency was producing enough raw footage but could not keep the publishing calendar full. Long-form recordings were stacking up, and the accounts were going days without a post while someone found time to edit.

## What I run

A fixed daily cycle that does not depend on anyone remembering to do it.

- **Morning block.** Cut, caption, and export the day's first batch. Publish the scheduled morning slots.
- **Afternoon block.** Prepare the evening batch and load it into the queue with captions and cover frames already set.
- **Evening block.** Publish, confirm both platforms accepted the upload, and write down anything the algorithm treated unusually.

Two videos per account per day is the floor, not the target. When a video performs, the next day's batch leans into the same hook structure.

## How the queue is tracked

Everything lives in one board: account, slot time, platform, and status from cut through published. Nothing gets posted because someone remembered. The board is the reminder.

## Account confidentiality

These are white-label client accounts, so I do not publish the handles. I can walk through the live board and the actual workflow on a call.

## What I do not claim

I do not have permission to publish reach or follower numbers for these accounts, so I do not put any here. The throughput above is what I control directly.
